# Geometry-Informed Reconstruction of Pothole Surfaces

**Research project title:**  
**Geometry-Informed Reconstruction of Pothole Surfaces via a Depth Function and Monotonic Neural Networks**

**Presented at:** Current Topics in Mathematics and Industrial Applications (CTMIA 2026)  
**Venue:** Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), VNU-HCM  
**Conference dates:** August 13–14, 2026

### Role

- Research collaborator
- Speaker / presenter
- Organizing Committee Member

### Research objective

The project explored reconstruction of pothole surface geometry to help autonomous robots navigate safely over damaged road surfaces.

### Technical idea

Instead of relying only on expensive 3D sensing hardware, the approach explored combining:
- RGB camera data
- Sparse 2D LiDAR data
- A depth function
- Monotonic neural networks

The goal was to reconstruct surface geometry with lower-cost sensing.
