# Responsibilities and Testimonials

The owner requested bullet points and selective emphasis for each company role. Each experience record now stores separate responsibility items and phrases to highlight. Employment disclosures and the company explorer use the same list renderer, so their wording and emphasis remain consistent. Highlight only existing technical contributions and reported metrics; do not add new achievements.

Testimonials is a visible Home section after skills and before contact. This placement is a design inference: visitors first see work and capabilities, then collaborator perspectives, then the contact action. It preserves the owner's five-page structure and keeps personal content on Home.

Research: [Nielsen Norman Group on trust in business websites](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/b2b-trust-from-b2c/) highlights the importance of testimonial authors' job titles and affiliations. [Trustworthiness in web design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/) also describes skepticism toward curated quotes. The existing Warm Ivory design system controls typography, colors, and surfaces; no new palette or stock portraits were introduced.

The owner will draft real recommendations later. Existing generic quotes are retained as unpublished drafts, excluded from the displayed section. Until approved quotes and named authors arrive, show a concise coming-soon state. Publishing a quote requires `published: true` and the owner's supplied wording and attribution. The prepared card layout shows quote, author, and organization without autoplay or a carousel.
